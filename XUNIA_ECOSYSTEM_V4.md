# THE RED HOUSE // XUNIA Ecosystem v4

The Red House is the canonical **authorized-security plane** in XUNIA.

```text
BLACK HOUSE / GPT-DOUG-LLM MAX
          ↓
       ZYRA GATE
          ↓
      RED HOUSE
assessment → findings → risk → remediation
          ↓
     EVIDENCE RETURN
```

Its job is to convert explicitly authorized security testing into structured findings, ontology relationships, prioritized risk, reproducible evidence and reviewable fixes.

The wider ecosystem collectively covers the six conceptual AI layers: Classical AI, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Deep Learning, Generative AI and Agentic AI. Red House consumes only the capabilities appropriate to authorized defensive work.

Canonical registry: `sonoxo/gpt-doug-llm/ecosystem/registry.v4.json`.

**Authorization boundary:** owned or explicitly authorized targets only. A repository link, ecosystem label, model decision or cross-house relationship never creates permission to assess a third party.
